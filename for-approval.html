Rules and setup for approval

State 26.10.2026. What changed in the modelling rules and in the setup, for the user to approve, change or reject. Approved items lose their "proposed" mark in the guideline (scan2revit/skills/modeling/SKILL.md); rejected ones are taken out, with the reason in documentation/decisions.md.

1. Modelling rules

RuleStateWhat it saysWhy
G-0.2 additionset by the user 26.10.2026The analysis of drawings and documents looks first for the construction: materials, structure, thicknesses and layers of slabs and walls.Asked by the user.
G-0.3 Independent sourcesproposedSources derived from each other are one source: drawings or models made from the scan are information (names, units), never a check of the scan. Every source says where it comes from (design, construction, survey, derived from the scan).The test house's plan set was drawn from the model of its point cloud: it cannot verify the scan.
G-9.2 Room separation linesset by the user 26.10.2026; wording to confirmWalls with their doors and openings bound a room. A separation line only (1) where a room does not close otherwise: one short line across the gap (passage, wide opening, a wall piece the scan did not see); (2) where the input divides a space without a wall (a foyer drawn as "Entrance" and "Foyer"). Never along whole room outlines, never where a wall stands, never more lines than it takes. A room that still does not close is listed for the person.The rule of 25.10.2026 drew a line along every room edge outside a wall: 1240 lines on a four-storey house, most of them useless. The code still follows the old rule: it is rebuilt to the new one once the rule is confirmed.
G-6.5 Slab inside the buildingproposedA slab never reaches beyond the building. A scan holding inside and outside together is split by the exterior walls; the outside points are used as the external scan (G-6.3: outline at the facade). Floor points outside the exterior walls (terrace, terrain) are not slab; a balcony or terrace slab is its own element and is listed.The house build's slabs ran out over terrace and garden in ragged shapes.
G-8.3 ColumnsproposedFree-standing columns found in the scan become column elements (structural families first, else architectural) of their shape, rectangular or round, at their place and rotation, from their level to the storey height; a column's faces are not also walls.Columns from scans are built now (F8); the checks use this rule.
G-11.7 Column type namesproposedscan2revit column <w> x <d> mm, scan2revit column round <d> mm, to 10 mm.Same naming as walls and floors.
G-11.8 Model file nameproposed<project>_v<setup>_<info>.rvt, e.g. house_v0.8.0-9459e42_scan-only.rvt.Asked by the user: project, version and short info in the file name.
G-13.5 Build registerproposedEvery build is recorded with its setup (Builds page). A later setup never overwrites a build: a rebuild gets a new file next to the old one; test models are kept the same way.Asked by the user: builds listed with the setup version they were built with.
