Drawings and scans together: 2D information as the prior for modelling

State 24.10.2026, plan; nothing of it is built yet. Decided by the user on 24.10.2026: the 2D drawings are analysed first and act as the prior; the scan measures the geometry and wins on geometry; the drawings win on what a scan cannot see. Every extracted piece of information is a fact in facts.json per project; a person verifies the facts on a review page at gate 1 before the builders use them. Decisions: decisions.md; the local LLM's part: local-llm.md; the feature list: open-tasks.md (F17 to F24).

1. Why

A scan sees surfaces: wall faces, floor tops, ceiling undersides, openings where they are open. It does not see what is inside a wall or slab, behind furniture, above a suspended ceiling, the names of rooms, or the build-up of a floor. Drawings carry exactly that, but they show the design, not necessarily the building as it is. So:

QuestionWho answers itWhy
Where is a wall face, a floor top, an opening edgescanmeasured; drawings may be outdated
How thick is a wall seen from one side onlydrawing (verified), else assumedthe scan sees one face
What is a slab made of, how thick is the structural slabsection or build-up legendthe scan sees only the finished surfaces
Storey heights, levels (OK FFB, OK RFB, UK RD)scan where seen; drawing for the rest and as a checksections carry every level, scans only what was scanned
Room names, numbers, units, usedrawing (room stamps, room schedules)not in a scan
Door and window sizes, sill and head where the scan saw nonedrawing (labels, elevations, schedules)a closed door or a slice route shows no head
Walls the scan missed (behind furniture, dense floors)drawing as hypothesis, scan as testthe drawn wall tells the detector where to look

Conflicts beyond the tolerance of a fact type (table 3) go to a person; the model never quietly takes the drawing over a measurement.

3. Facts: what is stored, and how

One file per project: scan2revit/data/projects/<project>/project/facts.json (git-ignored like all project data):

``json { "schema": "scan2revit.facts/1", "sources": [ {"id": "S1", "file": "A60.pdf", "sha256": "...", "kind": "plan", "pages": [0], "scale": 100} ], "alignment": {"source": "S1", "drawing": "EG", "to": "scan frame", "rotation_deg": 0.0, "translation": [0.0, 0.0], "rms_m": 0.0, "status": "proposed"}, "facts": [ {"id": "F-0042", "type": "buildup.floor", "subject": {"level": "1.OG"}, "value": {"layers": [["Parkett", 0.015], ["Unterlagsboden", 0.07], ["Trittschall", 0.02], ["Beton", 0.22]], "total": 0.325}, "unit": "m", "where": {"source": "S3", "page": 0, "bbox_pt": [412, 220, 530, 290], "crop": "crops/F-0042.jpeg"}, "method": "V-text", "confidence": 0.9, "status": "proposed", "verified_by": null, "verified_at": null, "note": "", "conflicts": []} ] } ``

  • status: proposed, accepted, corrected (value replaced by the person, the original kept in note), rejected. The builders use accepted and corrected facts only; proposed facts are listed as open.
  • confidence: from the reader (a clean pattern match high, an LLM answer at most 0.6); it orders the review.
  • conflicts: filled after the scan is read: {"with": "scan", "measured": ..., "difference": ..., "decision": null}. Tolerances per fact type (start values, decision D-6): levels 3 cm, wall thickness 2 cm, opening width 5 cm, sill and head 5 cm, room area 3 %, slab total 3 cm.
  • alignment: drawing coordinates to the scan frame, one per storey drawing: 2D fit of drawn wall axes to the scan's wall axes (rotation, translation, scale check against the sheet scale); verified like a fact.
  • plan.json carries references, not copies: an element built with a drawn value gets "fact": "F-0042" and its source flag (section 6).

4. Verification by a person: the review page

A local tool (scan2revit/tools/facts_review.py, to build) serves a review page on localhost for one project:

  • one card per fact: the crop of the drawing where it was read, the value, the method and confidence, and for a conflict the measured value next to it;
  • per card: accept, correct (enter the value), reject; bulk accept for one fact type above a confidence;
  • order: conflicts first, then low confidence, then the rest by type;
  • every decision is written into facts.json (verified_by, verified_at) and logged on the scoreboard (S1) as an action of the person;
  • the project manager holds gate 1 until no fact of the types the build needs is still proposed.

The page runs only on this machine and reads the project folder; nothing leaves it (customer drawings).

6. Measured, drawn, assumed

The modelling guideline knows measured and assumed (section 2, scan2revit source). With drawings a third source appears: drawn (taken from an accepted fact). Proposal for the guideline (decision D-4, not yet in SKILL.md): scan2revit source = measured, drawn: <what> (F-0042), assumed: <what>; a drawn value never replaces a measured one without a conflict decision.
